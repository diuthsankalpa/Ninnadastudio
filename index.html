Our Story

Located in the heart of Mathugama, Sri Lanka, Ninnada Studio is a premier creative hub dedicated to transforming musical visions into sonic reality. Founded and led by producer Sudarshana Akalanka, our studio blends a modern studio aesthetic with world-class production standards.

From high-fidelity recording and original track composition to professional mixing and mastering, we provide a complete ecosystem for both emerging and established artists. Whether you are looking to record your first cover or produce a full-scale original album, our mission is to bring your ideas to life through music that inspires, connects, and resonates on a global stage.
